You are NOT judging the garment's shape, category, fit, neckline, buttons, length or the

The crops exist so you can see the stripe at its real scale. Judge the pattern from the CROPS.
The full frames are context for where the crop came from.

BOTH BEING "STRIPED" IS NOT ENOUGH. That is the failure this examination exists to catch. A
source with fine dense stripes and a generated garment that reads as nearly plain, faintly
striped, broadly striped, differently spaced or differently coloured is a DIFFERENT PATTERN,
and therefore a different SKU, even though both are technically striped.

Compare these properties, one at a time, and describe what you actually see in each image:

- presence      : is the stripe clearly visible on the generated garment at all, or has it
                  faded toward plain / near-solid?
- orientation   : vertical, horizontal or diagonal, in each?
- density       : roughly how many stripe repeats span the visible torso in each? A source
                  with about 5 repeats across the chest and a generated garment with about 20
                  is a different fabric.
- spacing       : the gap between stripes — even, or grouped with wide gaps between clusters?
- relativeWidth : are all the stripes the same width, or do thick and thin lines alternate?
                  If the source alternates thick and thin and the generated garment is
                  uniform, that is a FAIL.
- dominantColors: name the actual stripe colours in each. Two distinct colours becoming one,
                  or a saturated colour washing out to a tint of the ground, is a FAIL.
- repeatScale   : how wide is one full repeat relative to the garment?
- character     : fine/dense pinstripe, medium, or broad/bold banding?
- consistency   : does the stripe hold the same scale and spacing across the whole visible
                  torso, or does it drift, blur or dissolve in places?
- perceivedAsSameDesign : would a shopper comparing the two photographs believe they are
                  looking at the same fabric, or at a different colourway/pattern variation of
                  it? Answer FAIL if they would see a different design.

Folds, drape, lighting and perspective legitimately bend and foreshorten a stripe. A stripe
curving over a fold, or compressing near the side seam, is NOT a failure. A stripe with a
different width ratio, a different repeat count, a different colour or a different grouping IS
a failure, no matter how it is lit.

status for each property:
- "PASS"          you can see it in both and they match
- "FAIL"          you can see it in both and they materially differ
- "UNVERIFIABLE"  genuinely not visible in one of them. Never use this as a soft FAIL.

overallStatus is FAIL if any property FAILs.
