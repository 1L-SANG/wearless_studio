GATES

1. sku_fidelity
- Across every cut where the sold product is visible, it must remain the same SKU as that output's
  listed PRODUCT TRUTH: construction, material appearance, pattern placement/repeat, hardware,
  and text/logo.
- Camera angle and occlusion can hide evidence. Never call hidden evidence a mismatch, but if
  a required identity feature cannot be verified anywhere applicable, use UNJUDGEABLE.

2. target_color
- targetColor is an opaque grouping alias, not a human-readable color name. Do not infer hue from
  the alias text. Each output must match only the PRODUCT TRUTH selected by its own
  productTruthIndexes. Compare outputs with the same targetColor alias for cross-cut consistency.
- Allow physically plausible changes caused by shadow, highlight, reflected light, exposure,
  and white balance. Fail hue-family replacement, inconsistent colorways, or recoloring that
  cannot be explained by the depicted illumination.

3. model_continuity
- Compare only visible worn cuts that share the same non-null modelId alias. They must depict the
  same selected person: face when visible, plus stable body proportions, skin tone, and hair.
- Different modelId values are intentional and must not be forced to match.
- NOT_APPLICABLE is allowed only when no modelId occurs in at least two visible worn cuts.

4. matching_continuity
- Compare only cuts that share the same non-empty matchingIds alias set. The selected coordinating
  garments must keep their construction, color, material, pattern, hardware, length, fit, lettering,
  numbers, graphics, embroidery, and logos. Missing, garbled, simplified, invented, or reversed marks
  that should remain visible are continuity failures.
- Different matchingIds sets are intentional. Product-only cuts need no matching garment.
- NOT_APPLICABLE is allowed only when no matchingIds set occurs in at least two applicable cuts.

5. outer_inner_continuity
- When two or more worn cuts visibly show the inner garment under the sold outerwear, that inner
  garment must remain the same item and color unless the plan's matchingIds differ.
- Judge only what is visible. NOT_APPLICABLE is allowed only when fewer than two cuts expose an
  inner garment enough to compare. If two applicable views exist but evidence is unusable, use
  UNJUDGEABLE, not NOT_APPLICABLE.

6. space_continuity
- Judge location/light continuity ONLY within cuts sharing the same non-null spaceGroupId alias.
- Within each such group, results must look like different frames from one real photo session:
  recognizable repeated architecture/materials/layout and physically coherent light direction,
  softness, temperature, contact/cast shadows, exposure, and white balance.
- Real camera movement is expected. New perspective, scale, parallax, framing, and newly revealed
  parts of the same location are valid; warped or re-cropped duplicates and unrelated places fail.
- Do NOT require all page cuts to share one place. Different spaceGroupId values and ungrouped cuts
  may use entirely different locations and lighting.
- NOT_APPLICABLE is allowed only when no spaceGroupId contains at least two outputs.

7. completeness
- There must be one usable generated image for every PAGE PLAN entry, in mapped order: no missing,
  blank, corrupt, accidental collage/grid, or unrelated output.
- A product-only cut may omit a person. A crop may hide features outside its intended framing;
  neither is incomplete by itself.
