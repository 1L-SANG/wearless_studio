You are given ONE finished mannequin photo. The mannequin wears a main top-or-outerwear garment
together with a bottom garment.

Your ONLY task: make sure the main garment's hem hangs OUTSIDE the bottom, all the way around
the body. Everything else in the photo is already correct and must survive untouched.

<the task>
Look at where the main garment's hem meets the bottom's waistband.

- If any part of the hem disappears behind or under the bottom's waistband — a full tuck, a
  front or half tuck, one side tucked, or the hem folded or rolled under — PULL IT OUT. Redraw
  the hem so it hangs down over the waistband and forms one unbroken visible line around the
  entire body.
- The tell that a tuck exists: any part of the bottom's waistband, drawstring, ties, buttons or
  zip drawn IN FRONT OF the main garment. In the corrected photo the main garment is the outer
  layer where the two garments overlap, and it covers those elements.
- If the hem already hangs fully outside with an unbroken line, the photo is already correct —
  return it unchanged.
</the task>

<what must not change>
- The main garment's FIT, ease, silhouette, drape and overall length. Pulling the hem out will
  reveal fabric that was hidden; draw that fabric at the garment's own natural length — do not
  stretch, shorten or reshape the garment beyond what un-tucking requires.
- The garment's fabric: colour, pattern, stripe or check scale, texture, buttons, pockets, seams.
- The bottom garment: its colour, wash, length and shape stay exactly as they are — only how
  much of its waistband is covered changes.
- The mannequin's body, pose, hands, camera framing, crop, lighting and background.
</what must not change>

<critical rules>
- Output the same photo with the hem corrected. Not a new photo, not a new pose, not a new crop.
- One unbroken hem line visible around the entire body — front, sides and back edge where visible.
- No part of the bottom's waistband, drawstring or fastening may remain drawn over the main
  garment.
</critical rules>
