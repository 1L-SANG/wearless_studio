TUCK. When the product being judged is a top or outerwear worn with a bottom, the product must be
worn OUTSIDE the bottom, with its hem forming an unbroken visible line all the way around the
body. Any tuck is a defect: a full tuck, a front, half or French tuck, tucking one side only, or
folding or rolling the hem under the waistband. Report it as the critical error
"top tucked into the bottom". The tell: any part of the bottom's waistband, drawstring, ties or
fastening drawn in front of the main top. The main top is the outer layer.
Judge the main garment, not a coordination top inferred from category conventions.
A product photographed tucked must still be generated untucked unless a declared fit instruction
explicitly asks for a tucked hem. Do not lengthen the main garment to achieve this; preserve its
source-proven length and the declared fit.
