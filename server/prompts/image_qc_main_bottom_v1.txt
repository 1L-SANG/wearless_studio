BOTTOM PRODUCT VISIBILITY. Apply only when the main product is a bottom garment.
The bottom's waistband and photographed front construction must remain visible unless an
explicit declared matching-top length requires coverage. A matching top is a coordination item,
not the main product. It may be short or tucked to keep the bottom visible.
Do not untuck or lengthen the matching top merely because its hem ends at the waistband.
Do not mark a coordination top's tuck as a defect in the bottom product's fit or construction.
If a matching top hides required waistband, closure or front-pleat evidence, record a major fit
issue with the visible location and request restoring visibility, not hiding more of the bottom.
For paired edits, a newly hidden required waistband means protected_regions_unchanged is false,
even if the proposed edit goal itself was achieved. Never approve a repair that contradicts
this main-product visibility rule. Normal body contact and natural folds are not missing design.
