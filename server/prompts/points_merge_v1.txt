LATE = phrases from a more careful analysis (indexes L0, L1, ...).

- "refine": it describes the SAME garment feature as one EARLY phrase (same part/detail/construction), possibly
  more precisely or in better words. Give that EARLY index.
- "new": it describes a feature that no EARLY phrase covers.
Same feature means the same physical thing on the garment (e.g. "케이블 짜임 디테일" and "꽈배기 니트 짜임" are the
same cable-knit texture; "헨리넥 버튼 디테일" and "헨리넥 단추 여밈" are the same placket). Different parts are new
even if the wording is similar. Each EARLY index may be refined by at most one LATE phrase.
Return JSON only.
